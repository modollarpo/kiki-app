| **Ariyo Dolapo** | 70% of fully diluted ordinary shares |
| **UK Co-Founder** | 20% of fully diluted ordinary shares |
| **ESOP Pool** | 10% reserved for future key hires (issued from company, not existing holders) |
| **Vesting** | 4-year vesting, 12-month cliff for Co-Founder. Ariyo's shares are fully vested (IP and product pre-dates incorporation) |
| **Cliff** | If Co-Founder leaves within 12 months, 0% equity vests |

| **Good Leaver** | Vested shares retained at cost. Company has 90-day ROFR at market value (determined by independent valuation or most recent round) |
| **Bad Leaver** | Vested shares repurchased by company at lower of par value or cost. Unvested shares forfeited |
| **Pre-emption** | Pro-rata participation rights in future funding rounds (standard) |
