2.3 Leaver Provisions

Upon termination of Founder B's involvement:

Good Leaver:

  • Vested Shares: Retained
  • Unvested Shares: Forfeited to Company at par value
  • Company has 90-day ROFR over Vested Shares at price determined by:
    • Most recent funding round valuation, or
    • Independent valuation by a qualified accountant agreed by both Parties

Bad Leaver:

  • Vested Shares: Subject to compulsory transfer at lower of par value or £0.001 per share
  • Unvested Shares: Forfeited
  • Company has 30 days to exercise repurchase

4. Intellectual Property

4.1 Pre-Existing IP

All IP created prior to incorporation by Founder A (including KIKI Agent source code, AI models, agent architectures, designs, brand, and business plans) is assigned to the Company under a separate IP Assignment Deed executed concurrently with this Agreement.

4.2 Company IP

All IP created by either Founder during the term of this Agreement:

  • Is the sole property of the Company
  • Both Founders hereby assign all right, title, and interest in such IP to the Company
  • Both Founders shall execute all documents reasonably required to perfect such assignment

5.3 Day-to-Day Operations

Each Founder has authority to make operational decisions within their defined domain and within the approved budget without requiring the other's consent.

6. Funding & Dilution

6.1 Future Rounds

Both Founders shall have pro-rata participation rights in future funding rounds to maintain their ownership percentage.

6.2 Anti-Dilution

Standard weighted-average anti-dilution protection applies to all Share holders in the event of a down round.

6.3 Founder A Veto

Founder A retains veto power over any financing round or dilution event that would reduce Founder A's ownership below 50% of the fully diluted share capital.

6.4 Pre-Emption on New Shares

Before issuing any new Shares (outside the ESOP), the Company must offer them pro-rata to all existing Shareholders on the same terms.

7. Remuneration & Benefits

7.1 Base Salary

Neither Founder shall draw a salary until the earlier of:

  • Company achieving £300K annual recurring revenue (confirmed over 2 consecutive quarters), or
  • Closing of a Seed round of at least £750K

7.2 Expense Reimbursement

Reasonable business expenses reimbursed against receipts, capped at £2,000 per Founder per month until funded.

7.3 Dividends

Board may declare dividends when:

  • Company has been profitable for 4 consecutive quarters, and
  • Operating cash reserve exceeds 6 months' burn, and
  • Both Founders consent

7.4 ESOP

Upon incorporation, the Company shall establish an EMI-approved Employee Share Option Plan reserving 10% of the fully diluted share capital for grants to future employees, advisors, and consultants. The Board shall determine grant allocations.

8. Restrictive Covenants

8.1 Confidentiality

Each Founder shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence. This obligation survives termination indefinitely.

"Confidential Information" includes: source code, AI model weights and architectures, customer data, financial data, business plans, investor communications, system architecture, API designs, and trade secrets.

8.2 Company Property

Upon termination, each Founder shall immediately return all Company property, including laptops, documents, source code access, and credentials.

9. Dispute Resolution

9.1 Escalation

  1. Informal discussion between Founders
  2. Mediation via Centre for Effective Dispute Resolution (CEDR)
  3. Binding arbitration under the Arbitration Act 1996, London Court of International Arbitration rules

9.2 Mediation

If a dispute cannot be resolved within 14 days of informal discussion, either Founder may refer the matter to CEDR. The mediator shall be agreed within 14 days.

9.3 Arbitration

If mediation fails, either Founder may refer to binding arbitration. The arbitration award shall be final and binding.

9.4 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts for enforcement.

10. General Provisions

10.1 Entire Agreement

This Agreement (together with the IP Assignment Deed and Company Articles of Association) constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

10.2 Amendment

Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Founders.

10.3 Legal Advice

Each Founder acknowledges they have been advised to seek independent legal advice and either have done so or voluntarily waive that right.
