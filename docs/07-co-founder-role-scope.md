# KIKI Agent™ — UK Co-Founder: Role Scope

**A STOREGRILL INC LTD Company** — Registered in England & Wales

---

**Position:** Co-Founder, Operations & Revenue  
**Location:** UK (London preferred, remote within UK considered)  
**Reporting to:** Founder / Board  
**Status:** Full-time from Day 1  
**Compensation:** 20% equity (4-year vest, 1-year cliff), £0 salary until £300K ARR or Seed close

---

## 1. Why This Role Exists

KIKI Agent is a fully built, production-deployed B2B SaaS platform (v2.0.0, 75+ API routes, 6 AI agents, 42 dashboard surfaces, 14 connectors). The technology is complete. What the company needs now is operational and commercial execution.

The Founder (Ariyo Dolapo) built the entire platform alone and will continue to own product and technology. The Co-Founder will own everything else — taking KIKI from a solo-built platform to a revenue-generating, UK-domiciled, investor-backed company.

This is not a traditional COO hire. This is a founding role requiring hands-on execution across sales, compliance, fundraising, and operations. The Co-Founder will build the commercial function from zero.

---

## 2. Core Responsibilities

### 2.1 Sales & Revenue Operations (40% of time)

**Year 1 Objective:** 15 paying customers, £112K revenue, repeatable sales process documented

**Activities:**
- Own the entire sales pipeline from prospecting to close
- Develop ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) targeting UK D2C brands (£10M–£250M GMV)
- Build and manage outbound sequences (LinkedIn, email, cold calls)
- Run product demos and technical sales conversations
- Negotiate pricing and close deals (Growth: £599/mo, Scale: £1,799/mo)
- Design and implement a customer onboarding flow
- Build a referral programme for early customers
- Document sales playbook for future SDR/BDR hires

**Year 2 Objective:** 50 customers, £525K revenue, first sales hire trained

**Year 3 Objective:** 150 customers, £1.9M revenue, repeatable inbound + outbound engine

**Metrics:**
- Monthly qualified pipeline value
- Demo-to-close conversion rate (target: 25%+)
- Average deal size and sales cycle length
- Customer acquisition cost by channel

---

### 2.2 UK Entity & Regulatory Compliance (20% of time)

**Month 1–2 Deliverables:**
- Incorporate KIKI Agent Ltd at Companies House
- Appoint directors (Ariyo Dolapo + Co-Founder)
- Open UK business bank account (Wise Business / Starling / HSBC Kinetic)
- Register with ICO for data processing (annual fee: from £40)
- Appoint KIKI Agent Ltd as the data controller under UK GDPR
- Draft Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)
- Secure professional indemnity insurance (£1M min) and cyber liability insurance
- Set up accounting in Xero or FreeAgent
- Engage a UK solicitor for Founders' Agreement and IP Assignment Deed

**Ongoing:**
- Annual Confirmation Statement to Companies House
- Annual accounts filing (via accountant)
- VAT registration and quarterly returns (when turnover exceeds £90K)
- PAYE registration and payroll (when first employee hired)
- ICO annual renewal and data breach reporting procedures

---

### 2.3 Fundraising & Investor Relations (20% of time)

**Pre-Seed Round (£350K — Q3 2026)**
- Build investor target list (UK angels, SEIS-focused funds, ad-tech angels)
- Prepare investor materials (teaser, financial model, data room)
- Manage the fundraising process — outreach, meetings, follow-ups, due diligence
- Apply for SEIS Advance Assurance from HMRC (required before any SEIS-qualifying investment)
- Manage legal due diligence and subscription agreement process

**Seed Round (£1.5M — Q3 2027)**
- Build relationships with UK seed funds (LocalGlobe, Episode1, Seedcamp, M&G, Passion Capital)
- Prepare for institutional due diligence
- Support lead investor selection and term negotiation

**Investor Communications (ongoing)**
- Monthly investor updates (metrics, milestones, challenges)
- Board meeting preparation and materials
- Cap table management

**Metrics:**
- Investor meetings booked per week (target: 5–10)
- Conversion rate: meeting to term sheet
- Time to close (target: 3–4 months for pre-seed)
- Dilution management

---

### 2.4 Financial Operations & Strategy (10% of time)

- Manage cash flow and budget vs. actuals
- Process invoices, payments, and expense reimbursement
- Work with accountant on management accounts and tax filings
- Build and maintain financial model (update monthly with actuals)
- Pricing strategy — monitor market, adjust tiers, run experiments
- Revenue operations — invoice generation, payment collection (Stripe), dunning

**Metrics:**
- Cash runway (months)
- Budget variance (target: <10%)
- Days sales outstanding (target: <14 days)
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) and annual recurring revenue (ARR)

---

### 2.5 Customer Success & Support (10% of time)

**Year 1 (hands-on):**
- Onboard every new customer personally (setup, training, first enriched signal)
- Act as Tier 1 support during UK business hours
- Collect feedback for product roadmap
- Manage NPS surveys and reference-able customer programme

**Year 2+ (scaling):**
- Hire Customer Success Manager (CSM) when customer count exceeds 30
- Design escalation paths (Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Engineering)
- Build customer health scoring model

**Metrics:**
- Net Promoter Score (target: 40+)
- Customer activation rate (% who send first enriched signal within 7 days)
- Time-to-value (days from signup to first enriched signal delivery)
- Monthly churn rate (target: <3% Year 1, <2% Year 2+)

---

### 2.6 Partnership Development (Supplementary)

- Establish KIKI Agent as a Meta Business Partner (when revenue threshold met)
- Apply for Google Cloud Partner programme
- Explore agency reseller partnerships (white-label KIKI for agencies)
- Engage ad platform partner teams (Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn) for co-marketing

## 3. Ideal Candidate Profile

### Must-Have

| Requirement | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| 5+ years experience in ad tech, martech, or B2B SaaS | Must understand the market, buyer, and competitive landscape |
| UK-based (full-time, no timezone gap) | Company will be UK-domiciled; customers, investors, and regulators are UK |
| Sales / revenue experience — not just operations | KIKI needs revenue. First 50 customers come from founder-led sales |
| Existing UK D2C or agency network | Accelerates first customer acquisition by 3–6 months |
| Fundraising experience (or willingness to learn fast) | Pre-seed and Seed rounds are critical — no room for a learning curve |
| Full-time commitment from day one | This is not a side project. KIKI is at commercial inflection point |
| Comfortable with early-stage ambiguity | No established playbook, no existing team, no revenue yet |

### Strongly Desirable

| Attribute | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Understanding of digital advertising (Meta CAPI, Google Ads, programmatic) | Technical product. Sales conversations require credible domain knowledge |
| GDPR / data compliance experience | UK GDPR compliance is a legal requirement, not optional |
| UK SEIS/EIS knowledge | Every angel investor will ask about SEIS eligibility |
| Network in UK investor ecosystem | Warm introductions convert at 10× the rate of cold outreach |
| Experience building a sales process from zero | First sales hire will learn from the playbook the Co-Founder builds |

### Red Flags (Will Not Work)

- Cannot commit full-time
- Looking for a salary in Year 1
- No experience selling to marketers or advertisers
- Based outside the UK
- Has competing commitments (other startups, full-time job, consulting)
- Expects to "manage" rather than "do" in Year 1
